GETTING TO KNOW SHIBUYA

Popular Places to Visit

1

Shibuya Scramble Crossing

Shibuya Scramble Crossing is one of the best-known spots in Tokyo. It is always busy, especially when the lights change and people begin crossing the street.

2

SHIBUYA SKY

SHIBUYA SKY gives visitors a view of Tokyo from high above the city. On a clear day, you can see far across the skyline, making it a nice place to take photos.

3

Shibuya PARCO

Shibuya PARCO is a shopping center that is especially interesting for people who enjoy Japanese pop culture. It is worth visiting if you want to see something a little different while in Shibuya.
