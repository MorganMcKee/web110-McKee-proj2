A TASTE OF JAPAN

Discover Shibuya's Food

Trying local food is a great way to experience a new place. Shibuya has plenty of options for anyone wanting to try Japanese food during their visit. This page covers a few popular choices that are worth checking out.

Japanese Foods to Try

Here are a few foods you might want to try while visiting Shibuya.

Japanese Crepes

Japanese crepes are a popular treat in Tokyo, especially for people looking for something sweet. They are usually folded into a cone and filled with different toppings. Their shape also makes them easy to enjoy while walking around Shibuya.

Sweet Treat 🍓

Ramen

Ramen is a Japanese noodle soup that is especially nice when you want a warm meal. The noodles are served in a flavorful broth, with toppings that vary depending on where you eat. There are many ramen restaurants around Tokyo worth trying.

Warm & Savory 🍜

Yakitori

Yakitori is grilled chicken served on skewers and is a common choice at Japanese restaurants. It is often seasoned with salt or coated in a sweet and savory sauce. It is a good option if you want to try something different during your visit to Shibuya.

Grilled Favorite 🍢

A Little Food Tip!

If you have time, take a look around the streets near Shibuya's main attractions. You might find a restaurant that catches your interest. It is also a good idea to check its opening hours before visiting so you know when you can stop by.
